## Master Your Beats on DEX Ergy: How the VMS5 Revolutionizes American DJ Setups

[![American Audio VMS5 is PCDJ supported](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/vms5-cover.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/vms5-cover.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "American Audio VMS5 is PCDJ supported")

**Download the DEX 3 and DEX 3 RE map for the American Audio VMS5 DJ controller below!**

The American Audio VMS5 DJ controller ($399.99 USD) can now be used in plug-and-play fashion with both DEX 3 and DEX 3 RE [DJ programs](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/). Simply download the map file and follow the install instructions below and you’re ready to get into the mix!

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![American Audio VMS5 DJ Controller](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/VMS5-angle.jpg?fit=300%2C232&ssl=1 "American Audio VMS5 DJ Controller")](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/VMS5-angle.jpg?fit=1030%2C795&ssl=1)

The VMS5 is the latest edition of the VMS series of [DJ controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) from American Audio — and hands-down the best our team has mixed with. For performance minded DJs, especially mobile and wedding DJs, looking for a well-constructed, all-in-one DJ Controller to control [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) or [DEX 3 RE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) (2 Deck and 4 Deck Support) the VMS5 should be on your short list.

For DJs that use additional analog sources the VMS5 will also work as a stand-alone traditional mixer with 4 line inputs, 2 Phono inputs and 2 Microphone inputs. In addition, some general features include 4 USB / Analog selectable Midilog (Midi & Analog) channels, gain/treble/mid/bass EQ on all MIDILOG channels.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/VMS5-front.jpg?fit=300%2C44&ssl=1 "American Audio VMS5 Front")](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/VMS5-front.jpg?fit=1030%2C150&ssl=1)

Another nice feature of the VMS5 is Inno-fader compatibility (evidently the VMS Series of DJ controllers were the first to offer this) with curve control and assign and reverse, XLR master output with Balance & Booth output.

The VMS5 also includes 62 Assignable MIDI buttons and switches, dual 2000 point resolution touch sensitive turntable scratch controls, dual MIDI Controls on all buttons and switches, 5 assignable linear fader controllers, two 10-bit resolutions pitch sliders, and multi-functional rotary browser with 4 function buttons.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![American Audio VMS5 Back](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/VMS5-back.jpg?fit=300%2C44&ssl=1 "American Audio VMS5 Back")](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/VMS5-back.jpg?fit=1030%2C150&ssl=1)

For a DJ controller priced under $400 the VMS5 is truly loaded with useful and surprising functionality. We feel the DJ controller a great fit for mobile and wedding DJs using either DEX 3 or DEX 3 RE.

#### American Audio VMS5 Specifications:

**GENERAL FEATURES:**

* Midi Controller with 4 USB / Analog selectable MIDILOG™ (Midi & Analog) channels
* All audio control faders, knobs, switches, and buttons smoothed – eliminates popping, wiper noise, and zipper noise
* On/Off soft startup and turnoff
* Gain/treble/mid/bass EQ on all MIDILOG™ channels
* 2 phono/line selectable analog channels
* Two microphone channels with illuminated on/off switches and gain/treble/mid/bass EQs
* On/off cueing on each MIDILOG™ channel
* Split cueing headphones with rotary mix control
* Illuminated rubber knobs for ease of use in dark environments
* Solid 1.6mm thick faceplate for extra durability
* Panel mounted rotary knobs
* Full analog mixer operation if the computer crashes or with external power supply
* Innofader replaceable crossfader compatible (sold separately)
* Crossfader curve controlCrossfader assign and reverse
* Q-Start crossfader controlled startup for 2 external audio players
* Master and Booth outputs with L/R balance
* Balanced Master outputs
* 12 position stereo master output VU meter
* Dimensions (LxWxH): 19”x12.75”x1.5” / 482x325x40mm
* Weight: 13 lbs. / 6 kg.

**MIDI FEATURES:**

* Built-in mouse pad
* Long life MIDI assignable touch strips
* Two 10- bit resolution pitch sliders
* 62 assignable MIDI buttons and switches
* 50 MIDI buttons have LED illuminations, including 18 with dual blue/red LEDs
* 4 assignable rotary encoders
* 34 assignable rotary controllers
* 5 assignable linear fader controllers
* Dual MIDI controls on all buttons and switches
* Dual 2000 point resolution touch sensitive turntable scratch controllers

**AUDIO FEATURES:**

* 24-bit 48 kHz analog audio
* 16-bit 44.1kHz USB audio
* 10-bit resolution on all analog fader controls
* THD <0.1%\*
* Channel separation >60dB\*
* S/N ratio >85dB\*
* Analog channel audio latency < 1msec
* Selectable USB modes: 4 in 4 out mode for running software effects on phono/line analog channels or 2 in 8 out mode for running 4 software channels and master recording
* 2 msec response on all buttons
* 4 msec response on all rotary and linear faders
* 1 msec USB latency
