## Experience the Latest PartyTyme Karaoke on DEX 3.9 - Subscription Launch Demo

The video above demonstrates the new in-app **Party Tyme Karaoke Subscription** service coming to DEX 3.9 (releasing to the public soon).

Search, browse, stream, and download **over 13,500 high-quality karaoke songs** (and growing) new and old in all popular music genres. Stream from the karaoke library while connected to the internet or **download the entire catalog for offline use** at a venue. Everything is done directly in DEX 3’s browser, including signing up for the karaoke subscription service ($99.99-a-month or a day access pass can be purchased for $29.99).  

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Party Tyme Karaoke Subscription for DEX 3 karaoke software](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/DEX3-PartyTyme.jpg?fit=300%2C169&ssl=1 "Party Tyme Karaoke Subscription for DEX 3 karaoke software")](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/DEX3-PartyTyme.jpg?fit=1030%2C579&ssl=1)

The **Party Tyme Karaoke Subscription** service is **100% legal for commercial use** in bars and other venues so you can host karaoke with confidence. The current selection of karaoke songs is outstanding and **will continue to grow on a weekly basis** at no additional charge to subscribers. Freshly uploaded songs will automatically populate the catalog once you log in, no fuss!

DEX 3.9 will come equipped with both Pulselocker ([music subscription](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)) and Party Tyme Karaoke (karaoke subscription) making it the choice selection for versatile DJs and venues that want to offer both music and karaoke.
